## Design Context

### Users
Primary user is a solo reseller operator who also runs a small volume of credit-card cashback arbitrage orders for a buying group. They check Vision from both desktop and phone, often in fast, real-world contexts like parking lots, store aisles, and quick admin sessions at home. The product's job is to give them immediate money clarity, task clarity, and confidence about what needs action next.

### Brand Personality
Vision should feel sharp, capable, and operator-first. The tone is low-drama, high-signal, and quietly premium rather than flashy. Emotionally, the interface should create confidence, control, and momentum.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark mode is the default and already fits the product. The visual language should stay in the current dark, high-contrast Vision family with precise typography, teal-led product chrome, and selective warm accents when the UI is about rewards, money movement, or urgency. It should not feel like generic SaaS, neon gamer UI, or colorful consumer fintech candy.

### Design Principles
1. Put the money story first: profit, capital tied up, reimbursement gaps, and urgency should read instantly.
2. Reward density without clutter: surfaces can be information-rich, but hierarchy must stay calm and scannable.
3. Treat mobile as a real operating environment: one-handed use, high contrast, and fast tap targets matter.
4. Keep income streams distinct but cohesive: reselling and churning should feel like separate lanes inside the same cockpit.
5. Use accent color intentionally: teal for product structure, warm reward tones for cashback, and red only for risk or slippage.
